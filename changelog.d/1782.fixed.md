Fixed an intermittent packer-bake failure where the GCP TechVault/Linux image validators reported a listening port as down. `ss -tlnH | grep -q ':22'` under `set -o pipefail` could return non-zero when `grep -q` closed the pipe on its first match and `ss` took SIGPIPE writing the next line. The validators now capture `ss` output once and match it with a here-string, so a listener check cannot spuriously fail.
